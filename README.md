## 🏛️ Architecture & Engineering

I care about more than simply making an application work.

My development principles include:

- **Clean and modular architecture**
- **Separation of responsibilities**
- **Reusable components**
- **Well-designed database schemas**
- **Secure APIs**
- **Efficient database queries**
- **Avoiding unnecessary N+1 queries**
- **Caching where appropriate**
- **Proper authentication and authorization**
- **Validation at the right layers**
- **Reliable error handling**
- **Production-ready deployment**
- **Maintainable code over short-term hacks**

For larger products, I generally prefer starting with a **well-structured modular architecture** and introducing additional services only when the business and technical requirements justify them.
